Zero-Trust Browser blocks ads, trackers and cookie banners out of the box, and syncs your bookmarks, history and tabs end-to-end encrypted. Fast, clean pages, and nobody watching over your shoulder.

WHAT IT DOES
• Built-in ad, tracker and cookie-banner blocking, with lists that update weekly.
• Strips tracking tags from links, per-site JavaScript switch and fingerprinting protection.
• A privacy report: trackers blocked per site and per week.
• Fire button: one tap closes every tab and wipes the session.
• Private tabs that block screenshots, with an optional separate lock.
• Cookie auto-clear when a site's last tab closes, except sites you keep.
• Translate pages on the phone itself, with nothing sent to a translation service.
• Reader mode, read aloud, dark mode for pages and text size.
• Bookmarks with folders, reading list for offline, send a tab to another device.
• Tab groups and tab search, picture-in-picture and background audio.
• QR scanner and voice search, print or save as PDF, add a site to the home screen.
• Save a page to Zero-Trust Notes or as a "read later" task.
• Import bookmarks from Chrome or Firefox. Works with your password manager.

WHY ZERO-TRUST
• End-to-end encrypted: everything is encrypted on your phone before it syncs. We can't read it, and neither can anyone who breaks into the server.
• One private account for every Zero-Trust app, unlocked with your fingerprint or face.
• Works offline and syncs across your devices when you're back online.
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no data sold. Ever.
• Open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check what the app does.
• English and Hebrew, with full right-to-left support.

Part of the Zero-Trust suite: Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Phone, Fitness, Meds, Time and Browser. One account opens them all.
